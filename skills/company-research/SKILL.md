# Company Research
1. What does it sell and who pays?
2. Business model and revenue drivers.
3. Customer value and switching costs.
4. Competitive advantage and durability.
5. Growth quality.
6. Margins, capital intensity, cash generation.
7. Share count, dilution, per-share economics.
8. Risks and thesis breakers.
9. Temporary vs structural issues.
10. Prefer filings and company disclosures.
11. Discuss valuation after understanding business quality and downside.
