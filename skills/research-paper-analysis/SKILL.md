# Research Paper Analysis
1. State the problem.
2. Explain why it matters.
3. Give intuition before equations.
4. Explain method/architecture.
5. Review experiments/evaluation.
6. Summarize strongest evidence.
7. Identify assumptions and limitations.
8. Compare alternatives.
9. Explain real-world transferability.
10. End with builder takeaways.
