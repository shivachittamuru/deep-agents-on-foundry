# Evidence Synthesis
1. Identify the decision/question.
2. Prioritize primary sources.
3. Track freshness/relevance.
4. Separate evidence from inference.
5. Identify agreement.
6. Identify disagreement.
7. Explain reasons for disagreement.
8. Avoid averaging incompatible claims.
9. State uncertainty.
10. Synthesize for the user's decision.
